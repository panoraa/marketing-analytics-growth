 Marketing Analytics & Growth Intelligence

An end-to-end marketing analytics project focused on understanding acquisition performance, customer behavior, conversion, and growth opportunities.

Project Overview

This project demonstrates how marketing and customer data can be transformed into actionable business insights.

The analysis covers:

* Marketing channel performance
* Customer acquisition
* Conversion funnel
* CAC and CPA
* ROAS and ROI
* Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
* Retention
* Cohort analysis
* Campaign performance
* Growth opportunities

Business Questions

The project answers questions such as:

1. Which marketing channels generate the highest-quality customers?
2. Which campaigns produce the strongest return?
3. Where are the largest conversion losses in the funnel?
4. Which customer segments generate the most value?
5. How does customer acquisition cost vary across channels?
6. Which channels have the strongest relationship between acquisition cost and customer lifetime value?
7. Where are the largest opportunities for improving marketing efficiency?
